Open issues

What is still open in the package and in the record, each with the page that gives the evidence and what closes it. Work that is registered or running (E45, E48, E46, E47) is listed on the experiments index; each experiment page states what its record does not hold.

Fits that do not finish one E-step

In V6 225 of 927 fits and in V7 123 of 992 ended on the 22-hour clock with zero EM iterations, after growing their draws to 2400 in the first E-step; they hold 95% (V6) and 94% (V7) of the fit-hours spent. None is a cr or dd fit. Their share rises with tree size (above 60% beyond 150 tips) and, at equal size, as \(\rho\) falls. Together with fits on the iteration cap, they leave the lineage-level models without a reading in V3, E46, V6, V7 and V9. Closed by a change to the E-step that lets these fits finish (or fail fast with a recorded reason), measured on the stalled trees, and an amendment that relaunches the series under it.

The conditioned fit's trace is its last segment

Under cond = "surface", estimate_rates() returns the last 25-iteration segment as details$mcem; the full trace is under conditioning. V3's reference statistics were taken from the former, so its rule 2 is not read. Closed by the package returning the full trace (or documenting where it is) and V3's references rerun.

E46's clock exceeds the cluster's allocation

E46's clock (four times the longest of a cell's first three trees) is above Bre's 24-hour limit on 547 of the 551 fits that ended on it, so "clock-limited" there records the allocation, not the design. Closed by an amendment that sets a clock the host can give, or a host that gives the registered one.
