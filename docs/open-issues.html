Open issues

What is still open in the package and in the record, each with the page that gives the evidence and what closes it. Work that is registered or running (E45, E48, E46, E47) is listed on the experiments index; each experiment page states what its record does not hold.

The unconditioned start takes the clock of a lineage-level fit

In V6 225 of 927 fits and in V7 123 of 992 ended on the 22-hour clock with iterations = 0; none is a cr or dd fit, their share rises with tree size (above 60% beyond 150 tips) and, at equal size, as \(\rho\) falls, and they hold 95% (V6) and 94% (V7) of the fit-hours spent. They did not stall in a first E-step. A fit under cond = "surface" runs an unconditioned start to the stopping rule, then the conditioned continuation in 25-iteration segments, and iterations counts the continuation only: under mc_error the start ran to its rule at up to 2400 draws and took a median of 10–19 hours of the clock, so the continuation never began. E46's stage columns say the same of every lineage-level model (the start alone a median of 0.7–24 hours; nd 23.6, nk 7.5, ned 3.5), and the start ends with the extinction coefficients on the edge of the box, where the proposal's effective sample is a few draws of thousands. Together with fits on the iteration cap, these leave the lineage-level models without a reading in V3, E46, V6, V7 and V9. Since emphasis b21083f the start gets at most a quarter of max_time and every stage is recorded (conditioning$runs); on the probe tree (48 tips, \(\rho = 0.5\), a 3-hour clock) the continuation then ran 191 iterations where the previous build spent the three hours in its start and recorded 0; the effective sample there is 1–14 of 1013 draws, so that cell is sampler-limited whatever the stopping rule. Closed by the measurement on six stalled V6 trees under b21083f (running) and the amendments of 2026-11-07 and 2026-11-08 that relaunch the lineage-level fits of V3, V5/E46, V6, V7 and V9 under it: V3 and V7 are running (V7 under the package's default rule projected, provisional until V3 reads that rule); V6 and V9 follow under the rule V3 supports.

The conditioned fit's trace is its last segment

Under cond = "surface" the package returned the last 25-iteration segment as details$mcem, and V3's reference statistics were taken from it, so its rule 2 is not read. Since emphasis b21083f details$mcem is the whole continuation's trace with its segment number. Closed by V3's references rerun under it.

E46's clock exceeds the cluster's allocation

E46's clock (four times the longest of a cell's first three trees) is above Bre's 24-hour limit on 547 of the 551 fits that ended on it, so "clock-limited" there records the allocation, not the design. Closed by an amendment that sets a clock the host can give, or a host that gives the registered one.
