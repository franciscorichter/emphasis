Open issues

What is still open in the package and in the record, each with the page that gives the evidence and what closes it. Work that is registered or running (E45, E48, E46, E47, the V series) is listed on the experiments index; each experiment page states what its record does not hold. The fits that spent their clock in the unconditioned start (V6, V7; amendment of 2026-11-07) are resolved in emphasis b21083f and measured on six stalled trees (V6, Six of them refitted). V3, V7, V6 and V9 run under the package's default rule projected, a fit on the 1000-iteration cap read as the estimate (amendment of 2026-11-08, evening; V3), and V5 (E46) from 10 November on two local hosts.

The conditioned fit's trace is its last segment

Under cond = "surface" the package returned the last 25-iteration segment as details$mcem, and V3's reference statistics were taken from it, so its rule 2 is not read. Since emphasis b21083f details$mcem is the whole continuation's trace with its segment number. Closed by V3's references rerun under it.

E46's clock exceeds the cluster's allocation

E46's clock (four times the longest of a cell's first three trees) is above Bre's 24-hour limit on 547 of the 551 fits that ended on it, so "clock-limited" there records the allocation, not the design. Closed by an amendment that sets a clock the host can give, or a host that gives the registered one.
