Question

Extinction is read from absences. Whether a tree identifies it depends on turnover and size; the conditioned fits of E45 and E46 and the exact map of E38 already hold the answer, once read together.

Design

inputs E45's cr, dd fits (25–800 tips; turnover 0.10, 0.25, 0.40); E46's extinction intercepts and slopes; E38's exact profiles
new fits none

Pre-set rules

  1. Per model, turnover and size: is the extinction rate reliable (E46's rule 2)?
  2. Is a slope generated at 0 fitted at 0?
  3. Does MCEM's extinction estimate track the exact one?

Results

Rscript sim/V10.R; tables in results/V10/ (rate.csv, slope0.csv, track.csv, reading.txt). The design fixes no threshold for rules 2 and 3, so they are reported as distributions.

Rule 1, the extinction rate by V5's rule 2 (median ratio in [0.8, 1.25], central 80% within [0.5, 2]):

model cells where it holds where
cr, exact and MCEM 4 of 24 turnover 0.40: 200, 400 and 800 tips at \(\rho\) 1, 200 at \(\rho\) 0.8
dd, exact (repaired reference) 0 of 19 median ratio 0.05–0.33
dd, MCEM 0 of 19
eight lineage-level models, MCEM (V5, V5b) 0 of 80 median ratio 0.004–1.47, 10th percentile at most 0.70

Under cr the exact estimate is zero on 10–50% of trees at turnover 0.10 (50% at 25 tips, 10% at 800), 0–32% at 0.25 and 0–20% at 0.40.

Rule 2, slopes generated at 0. Under dd the exact \(\gamma_N\) has a median of +0.006 to +0.12 and lies on its \(+3/n\) cap on 32–98% of the trees of a cell; its central 80% contains 0 in 11 of 19 cells, those of 100 tips and more. Of the lineage-level slopes, the central 80% contains 0 in every cell for \(\gamma_D\) (d, nd), centred ED (edc, nedc) and \(K\) (k, nk), with 0–37% of fits on a box edge. Raw ED is fitted negative: under ed the median \(\gamma_{ED}\) is the lower edge, −0.40, in 6 of 10 cells, a median 97% of fits are negative, and the central 80% contains 0 in 4 of 10; under ned the median is −0.04 to −0.40, with 21–59% of fits on the edge.

Rule 3, MCEM against the exact estimate. Under cr, where the exact \(\mu\) is positive, the median ratio of MCEM to exact is 0.93–1.07 in every cell, with a rank correlation of 0.94–1.00. Where the exact \(\mu\) is zero (0 to 20 of 40 trees a cell), MCEM returns a positive rate, a median 0.02–0.35 of its \(\lambda\). Under dd the ratio of the cell medians of MCEM and exact ranges from 0.16 to 55, so MCEM does not track the exact extinction estimate. The dd MCEM ratios are on the trees valid under the first exact reference, a subset of those of the repaired one.

Conclusion

Extinction is read from these trees only under constant rates at turnover 0.40 and 200 tips or more, by both MCEM and the exact estimate. Under diversity dependence and every lineage-level model it is read in no cell, and an extinction slope generated at 0 sits on a box edge on a large share of fits, toward negative values for raw ED.
