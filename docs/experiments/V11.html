Question

The real-tree results the biology paper reports must come from the protocol the series validates.

Design

trees E40's 175 (116 class-tree clades, 59 Condamine families)
fits the ladder's five models, conditioned, mc_error, told the tree's sampling fraction
jobs 875

Pre-set rules

  1. Registered to be submitted once V1–V10 are read; submitted on 11 November under the protocol settled on 8 November, because V7's first reading (the conditioned ladder names ned on 2 of 52 simulated trees with an ED effect, nk on 12 of 20 in the one cell read) puts in question the ned and nk choices the unconditioned fits made on the real trees (E40: 51 and 67 of 170). A change of protocol V5–V10 call for reruns it.

Amendment of 19 November (V11b)

The nd fit on its default thinning proposal is unread for an effective sample below 10 on 93 of 175 trees (final effective sample below 10 on 101; median 7.4). Every tree's nd fit is refitted with the surrogate proposal (control$sampling = "bdi"), otherwise as above, with an 18-hour clock, and the 10 fits the cluster stopped (nine nk, one ned) are refitted as above with a 44-hour clock (sim/V11b.R, results/V11b). V5's gate measured 143 to 620 effective draws with the surrogate on simulated nd trees of 200 tips, where thinning keeps 2 to 8. The reading replaces each tree's nd row by its V11b row and adds the refitted rows; everything else is V11's reading. The reading with the surrogate nd is the one the papers report; the reading with the thinning nd stays recorded as the registered one and is given beside it.

Results

Read 21 November (sim/V11-analysis.R --v11b, sim/V11-report.R --dir results/V11b; tables in results/V11b/). The 10 refits on the 44-hour clock are not yet in; their trees are counted as incomplete. A fit is read when it ended converged or on the 1000-iteration cap with a finite log-likelihood and a final effective sample of at least 10 (the protocol of the amendment of 2026-11-11); a tree is read when its five ladder fits are; the choice is the fewest parameters within 2 AIC of the minimum.

Fits. 697 of 865 read: cr 175 of 175, dd 168, ned 143, nk 108, nd 103. The nd fits on the surrogate have a median final effective sample of 13.0; 102 of the 103 read ones end on the iteration cap. The unread fits are at an effective sample below 10 (139: nd 66, nk 45, ned 24, dd 4), on the clock (19) or without a log-likelihood (10).

Trees. 63 read, 102 unread, 10 incomplete (a job without a row); 48 of the read trees are class-tree clades and 15 Condamine families. Of the 102 unread trees, nd fails on 67 (on 33 it is the only model that fails), nk on 57, ned on 31, dd on 6. The read trees have 50–196 tips (median 111), the unread ones 50–370 (median 116).

Choice on the 63 read trees: cr 4, dd 12, nd 2, ned 24, nk 21 — a lineage-level model on 47. By set: class-tree clades cr 2, dd 7, nd 1, ned 20, nk 18; Condamine families cr 2, dd 5, nd 1, ned 4, nk 3. By class, 51 of the read trees are birds (lineage-level on 44) and 12 mammals (on 3). Against E40's unconditioned choice on the same trees the choice is the same on 60 of 63; the three that differ move from cr to dd, from cr to nd and from nk to ned. \(\beta_K\) is positive on all 21 nk trees (median 0.140). A richer read fit lies more than 1 log-likelihood unit below the model it nests on 1 read tree.

Factors (speciation rate ratio per standard deviation of the covariate, over the read fits off the speciation edge of the box). \(K\): median 1.50, above 1 on 104 of 108 read nk fits; on the 21 trees choosing nk median 1.56, above 1 on all. ED: median 0.67, above 1 on 3 of the 109 with a factor; on the 24 trees choosing ned median 0.48, above 1 on none. \(D\): median 0.79, above 1 on 14 of 99; on the 2 trees choosing nd median 1.10. The \(K\) factor rises with the normalised Colless index (rank correlation 0.63 over 108 fits) and the ED factor falls with it (−0.58 over all 143 read ned fits, −0.49 over the 109 drawn in the figure). The trees choosing a lineage-level model have a median Colless index of 1.91, those choosing cr or dd 0.80.

The pairs. Both members are read on 3 of the 30 pairs (same choice on 1). Where a coefficient is read in both members its factor lies on the same side of 1 on 21 of 22 (\(D\) 5 of 6, ED 6 of 6, \(K\) 10 of 10).

Where the two readings differ. The nd fit is read on both samplers on 52 trees, on the thinning proposal only on 22 and on the surrogate only on 51; its median final effective sample is 7.4 on thinning and 13.0 on the surrogate. 37 trees differ in state or choice (36 change state; the Bovidae family tree changes choice). 25 are read only with the surrogate nd (thinning effective sample 1.0–7.5, surrogate 10.6–197.9), among them the Condamine families Phasianidae (ned), Scolopacidae (nk) and Troglodytidae (dd). 11 are read only with the thinning nd, whose surrogate fit keeps 1.3–8.3 effective draws: the Condamine families Corvidae (nk on thinning), Hirundinidae (nk), Laridae (nk), Leporidae (nk) and Monarchidae (dd), and the clades bird-051-Monarchidae, bird-065-Falconidae, bird-070-Tinamidae, mamm-015-Bovidae, mamm-034-Cricetidae and mamm-035-Nesomyidae. The Bovidae family tree is read in both and moves from nd (thinning) to dd (surrogate). The nk and ned fits are the same in both readings, so their factors and AIC comparisons do not change; only the choices of trees whose nd row changes do.

The thinning reading (V11, registered). Read 18 November (results/V11/): 668 of 865 fits read (nd 74); 49 trees read, 116 unread, 10 incomplete; nd fails on 93 of the 116 unread trees. Choice on the 49: cr 5, dd 9, nd 2, ned 17, nk 16, a lineage-level model on 35 (median Colless 2.59 against 0.67 for cr or dd); E40's choice kept on 46 of 49; \(\beta_K\) positive on all 16 nk trees (median 0.148); 17 family trees read; both members of 2 pairs read.

Descriptive, not the design's ladder (the four fits cr, dd, ned, nk, leaving out nd; the same in both readings): 96 trees have all four read; the choice is cr 6, dd 19, ned 33, nk 38, and E40's choice is kept on 90 of 96. The trees choosing ned or nk have a median of 124 tips, those choosing cr or dd 71.

What it does not settle. On simulated trees of about 100 tips the same ladder names a lineage-level model on 0 of 54 trees without one and misses most real effects (V7); on real trees it names one on 47 of 63 read trees. Whether those choices reflect a lineage-level process or a feature of real trees that the simulations lack (imbalance, the processes of V9) is not decided by V11.
