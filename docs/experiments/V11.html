Results

Read 18 November (sim/V11-analysis.R; tables in results/V11/). A fit is read when it ended converged or on the 1000-iteration cap with a finite log-likelihood and a final effective sample of at least 10 (the protocol of the amendment of 2026-11-11); a tree is read when its five ladder fits are; the choice is the fewest parameters within 2 AIC of the minimum.

Fits. 668 of 865 read: cr 175 of 175, dd 168, ned 143, nk 108, nd 74. The unread fits are at an effective sample below 10 (nd 93, nk 45, ned 24, dd 4), on the 22-hour clock (15) or without a log-likelihood (16).

Trees. 49 read, 116 unread, 10 incomplete (a job without a row). nd alone leaves 101 trees unread, nk 67, ned 32, dd 7 (a tree can fail on several). The read trees have 50–196 tips (median 109), the unread ones 50–370 (median 125).

Choice on the 49 read trees: cr 5, dd 9, nd 2, ned 17, nk 16 — a lineage-level model on 35. By set: class-tree clades cr 3, dd 5, ned 14, nk 10; Condamine families cr 2, dd 4, nd 2, ned 3, nk 6. Against E40's unconditioned choice on the same trees the choice is the same on 46 of 49 (cr 5, dd 9, ned 16, nk 16); the three that differ move from cr or dd to nd and from nk to ned. \(\beta_K\) is positive on all 16 nk trees (median 0.148). A richer read fit lies more than 1 log-likelihood unit below the model it nests on 3 read trees.

Descriptive, not the design's ladder (the four fits cr, dd, ned, nk, leaving out nd, whose sampler fails most often): 96 trees have all four read; the choice is cr 6, dd 19, ned 33, nk 38, and E40's choice is kept on 90 of 96. The trees choosing ned or nk have a median of 124 tips, those choosing cr or dd 71.

What it does not settle. On simulated trees of about 100 tips the same ladder names a lineage-level model on 0 of 54 trees without one and misses most real effects (V7); on real trees it names one on 35 of 49 read trees. Whether those choices reflect a lineage-level process or a feature of real trees that the simulations lack (imbalance, the processes of V9) is not decided by V11.

Amendment of 19 November (V11b)

The nd fit, on its default thinning proposal, has a final effective sample below 10 on 93 of 175 trees and alone leaves 101 trees unread. Every tree's nd fit is refitted with the surrogate proposal (control$sampling = "bdi"), otherwise as above, with an 18-hour clock, and the 10 fits the cluster stopped (nine nk, one ned) are refitted as above with a 44-hour clock (sim/V11b.R, results/V11b). A test fit on a 50-tip tree kept 191 effective draws of 200. The reading (sim/V11-analysis.R --v11b) replaces each tree's nd row by its V11b row; the reading above stays as the registered one.
