Question

Model choice is what a user reads first. Every earlier selection study ran on capped fits, the unconditioned likelihood or the old \(D\) likelihood (audit).

Design

cells the 100-tip E43 cells: 32; the 200-tip cells as a second wave
trees 20 a cell
fits the ladder's models (cr, dd, nd, ned, nk) and the generating model, one per job, conditioned, from the box's default start
readings the ladder's choice (fewest parameters within 2 AIC) and the pairwise choice (generator vs the model it nests)
jobs 3580

Pre-set rules

  1. False choice: on cr and dd trees a lineage-level model is chosen on at most 10%.
  2. Power: on each lineage-level generator's trees the ladder names the generator or a model containing its covariate on at least 80%.
  3. A cell with more than a tenth of fits on the cap or the clock is budget-limited, not read.

Results

Rerun (b21083f, projected): submitted 8 November; nothing read yet.

First pass (182055b, mc_error, results/V7-182055b), on the 992 fits of 5 November; not read against the rerun. "The cap" is the 1000-iteration limit (max_iter), "the clock" the 22-hour limit (time_budget); the tenth in rule 3 is of the planned fits (100 or 120 a cell).

Outcomes: 584 converged, 160 on the cap, 246 on the clock (123 of them with no conditioned iteration: the clock went to the unconditioned start, see Open issues), 2 E-step failures. Of the written fits, nd, ned and nk end on the cap or the clock on 91%, 80% and 90%.

Rule 3. 16 of 32 cells are budget-limited whatever the remaining fits do: both cr and both dd cells, all four d cells, nd t10 +0.35 and t40 +0.35, ed t40 −0.35, edc t40 +0.35, ned t10 −0.35, nedc t10 −0.35, k t10 +0.35 and t40 +0.35. Rule 1 (false choice) therefore cannot be read in any cell. The other 16 cells are 1–6 cap or clock fits away from the same state.

Ladders. 73 trees have all five ladder fits written; none has all five converged (converged share on those trees: cr 0.95, dd 0.84, nd 0.07, ned 0.15, nk 0.07).

Descriptive, not a rule reading. Pairwise choice on the 38 trees with both fits converged: the generator is chosen on 19 (d 4/8, ed 8/14, edc 4/5, k 2/7, nedc 1/2, ned 0/1, nk 0/1). On two trees a converged richer fit lies more than 1 log-likelihood unit below the model it nests (k t10 +0.35: k08 ned against dd, −1.45; k07 nd against cr, −1.02); fits start from the box default, not the nested estimate.
