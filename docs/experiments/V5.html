Question

From how many tips is each coefficient of the eight lineage-level models recovered under the conditioned likelihood, by the rules E46 fixed in advance. E46's first pass read 1 of 65 model–cell pairs; the fits that ended on the clock spent it in the unconditioned start (Open issues), which emphasis b21083f caps.

Design

cells every reachable E43 cell of d, nd, ed, edc, ned, nedc, k, nk at 50 and 100 tips (turnover 0.10 and 0.40, the coefficient at ±0.35): 29 at 50 tips, 28 at 100
trees 30 a cell, the series' trees (v_tree)
fit the generating model by estimate_rates(): defaults, conditioned on survival, the box of auto_bounds(), projected, ρ = 1, 22-hour clock
jobs 1,710
next the 200- and 400-tip cells for the parameters not reliable at 100 tips

Pre-set rules

E46's. A cell is read when at least 80% of its fits finished (converged or on the cap) and the median final effective sample is at least 10. A coefficient is reliable in a cell when the median ratio to its generating value lies in [0.8, 1.25] and the central 80% of ratios within [0.5, 2]; identified when reliable with the right sign on at least 95% of trees. The answer is the smallest size from which each coefficient is reliable.

Results

Interim, 23 November: 1,678 of 1,710 fits at 50 and 100 tips; 1,612 finished (246 met the stopping rule, 1,366 on the iteration cap, read as the estimate), 65 on the 22-hour clock. A cell is read when at least 24 of its 30 fits finished and its median final effective sample is at least 10: 54 of 57 cells. Table 6 of the methods paper (built by report/paper-tables.R) gives every cell as of 19 November.

  • Covariate slopes. Of 80 slope cells read, 21 meet rule 2, and all 21 are also identified (sign right on at least 95%). \(\beta_N\): at turnover 0.10 in 6 of 7 cells at 100 tips (nd and nedc at both signs, ned and nk at \(+0.35\)) and 4 of 8 at 50 (ned and nk at \(+0.35\), nedc at both signs); at turnover 0.40 in none of 11. \(\beta_{ED}\): ed (turnover 0.10 both signs, 0.40 at \(+0.35\)), edc (\(-0.35\) at both turnovers), all at 100 tips, and nedc at turnover 0.40 (\(-0.35\) at 100 tips, \(+0.35\) at 50); none in ned. \(\beta_K\): k at \(-0.35\) and nk at \(+0.35\), 100 tips, turnover 0.10. \(\beta_D\): d and nd at \(-0.35\), 100 tips, turnover 0.10.
  • Intercepts. \(\beta_0\) meets the rule in 24 of 54 cells, in none of the 11 cells of an \(N\) model at turnover 0.40. \(\gamma_0\) meets it in none; within a factor of 2 on a median 7% of a cell's fits.
  • Not read. k at \(+0.35\) (100 tips, turnover 0.10), and at turnover 0.40 and 100 tips ed \(-0.35\) and nedc \(+0.35\): budget-limited, 22 or 23 of 30 fits finished and the rest on the clock.

200 tips (V5b)

22 November: 448 of 450 fits written, 442 finished (the rest on the 44-hour clock); 14 of 15 cells read. d \(-0.35\) at turnover 0.40 is sampler-limited (median final effective sample 8.2). Of the 20 slope cells read, 8 are identified: \(\beta_D\) of d at both signs and of nd at \(-0.35\); \(\beta_N\) of nd at \(-0.35\) and of ned at \(+0.35\); \(\beta_{ED}\) of edc at \(-0.35\) and of ed at \(+0.35\) at both turnovers; all at turnover 0.10 except ed at 0.40. Against 100 tips, d \(+0.35\) is identified at 200 and not at 100, and nd \(+0.35\)'s \(\beta_N\) is identified at 100 and not at 200 (median ratio 1.00, 10th percentile 0.44). At turnover 0.40 nd's \(\beta_N\) has a negative median ratio, and only ed \(+0.35\) is identified. \(\beta_0\) meets the rule in 12 of 14 cells, \(\gamma_0\) in none.

400 tips (amendment of 21 November)

Eight cells pass the gate. By the registered wording (a parameter not reliable at 200) all eight qualify, because \(\gamma_0\) is reliable in none; the three fitted are those with a speciation coefficient not reliable at 200, a narrower criterion chosen with the 200-tip interim in hand (amendment of 25 November): nd \(+0.35\) and edc \(+0.35\) at turnover 0.10, d \(+0.35\) at 0.40. The other five were submitted on 25 November, so all eight are fitted. nd and edc \(+0.35\) ran on four threads a fit, where V5 and the 200-tip fits ran on one; their readings below are four-thread readings. The other six cells run on one thread.

  • nd \(+0.35\), read (30 fits, 29 finished). Both slopes are identified: \(\beta_D\) at a median ratio of 1.05 (10th to 90th percentile 0.55 to 1.42) and \(\beta_N\) at 0.92 (0.60 to 1.30), right sign on every tree; at 200 tips neither met the rule (\(\beta_D\) 0.29 to 1.82, \(\beta_N\) 0.44 to 1.33).
  • edc \(+0.35\), read (28 of 30 fits). \(\beta_{ED}\) is identified: median ratio 1.04 (0.76 to 1.41), right sign on every tree; at 200 tips it was 0.97 (0.41 to 1.22).
  • The six others (180 fits): queued.

The slopes fitted at 400 tips are those that missed the rule at 200, so part of the change between the two sizes can be sampling variation. \(\gamma_0\) is not reliable in either cell (median ratios 0.16 and 0.51).

The 200- and 400-tip cells (amendments of 18 November)

The gate (sim/V5g.R, results/V5g/gate.csv): on every reachable cell at 200 and 400 tips, the effective sample of 2,400 draws at the generating values on the 30 trees V5 fits, with the fit's proposal. With thinning, the default for d and nd, 7 of their 8 cells keep a median of 1.7 to 7.5 effective draws at 200 tips; the surrogate keeps 143 to 620 on every nd cell and 16 to 256 on every d cell. By the evening amendment d and nd are fitted and gated on the surrogate at 200 and 400 tips, and k at \(+0.35\) (median 50 and 55 tips at the 200-tip target) is not a 200-tip cell. Pass at 200 tips (15 of 28): d and nd (all 8, surrogate), ed \(+0.35\) (both turnovers), edc (turnover 0.10 both signs, 0.40 \(+0.35\)), ned (turnover 0.10 both signs). Pass at 400 tips (8 of 24): d (turnover 0.10 both, 0.40 \(+0.35\)), nd (turnover 0.10 both), ed \(+0.35\) and edc (turnover 0.10).

The 200-tip fits (sim/V5b.R, results/V5b): 450 fits on the 15 passing cells, 44-hour clock, array v5b on Bre.
