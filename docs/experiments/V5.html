Question

From how many tips is each coefficient of the eight lineage-level models recovered under the conditioned likelihood, by the rules E46 fixed in advance. E46's first pass read 1 of 65 model–cell pairs; the fits that ended on the clock spent it in the unconditioned start (Open issues), which emphasis b21083f caps.

Design

cells every reachable E43 cell of d, nd, ed, edc, ned, nedc, k, nk at 50 and 100 tips (turnover 0.10 and 0.40, the coefficient at ±0.35): 29 at 50 tips, 28 at 100
trees 30 a cell, the series' trees (v_tree)
fit the generating model by estimate_rates(): defaults, conditioned on survival, the box of auto_bounds(), projected, ρ = 1, 22-hour clock
jobs 1,710
next the 200- and 400-tip cells for the parameters not reliable at 100 tips

Pre-set rules

E46's. A cell is read when at least 80% of its fits finished (converged or on the cap) and the median final effective sample is at least 10. A coefficient is reliable in a cell when the median ratio to its generating value lies in [0.8, 1.25] and the central 80% of ratios within [0.5, 2]; identified when reliable with the right sign on at least 95% of trees. The answer is the smallest size from which each coefficient is reliable.

Results

Nothing read yet. The record of 8–9 November said E46 reran since 8 November; it was not submitted then, and V5 is its registration and run (the design, amendment of 2026-11-10).
