Pre-set rules

  1. Descriptive: median and 90th percentile of time per fit by model, size, proposal and stopping rule.

Results

Rscript sim/V12.R; 13,849 fits; tables in results/V12/ (cost.csv, reading.txt). Times are of the whole fit (unconditioned start, survival surfaces and MCEM), on one thread; fits on the clock count at the clock (22 hours, 44 at 200 and 400 tips). Simulated cells pool turnover, effect and sampling fraction.

Stopping rules at 100 tips (V3, median hours): projected 0.04 (cr) to 5.9 (ed, ned, nk); the reference run (1200 draws, 400 iterations) 0.9 to 12.7. V3's mc_error arm holds only its first 81 fits and rel_change has not run, so they are not compared here.

Real trees (175, 50–370 tips): under projected, cr 0.02 hours, dd 0.2, nd on the surrogate 3.7 (90th percentile 9.5), ned 3.8 (15.6), nk 2.6 (8.8). On the same trees cr takes 0.04 hours under rel_change and 1.7 under mc_error with draws grown to 2400 (V4).

Conclusion

A lineage-level fit takes hours where cr and dd take minutes, and its time grows with size: at 200 tips the 90th percentile of every lineage-level model reaches the clock.
