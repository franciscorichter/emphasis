The validation series V1–V12

Registered 2026-10-31 from the report/experiment-history.pdf: of 56 experiments, 11 stand, 24 stand in part, 20 no longer stand and E46 is running. Each method the two papers use is validated by one experiment below, on one build (emphasis 182055b), under one protocol (00-design.md, The validation series): E43's calibrated generators with no size window, the conditioned likelihood, the exponential link, fits run to the stopping rule.

The lineage-level models have no reading in V3, V5, V6, V7 or V9: their fits end on the iteration cap or the 22-hour clock, many of them with the clock spent in the unconditioned start that precedes the conditioned segments (see Open issues). Under the amendments of 7 and 8 November their fits rerun on emphasis b21083f under the package's default rule projected: V3 (its ref, proj and mcfix arms; rel and mc later), V7, V6 and V9 are all submitted. Since the evening amendment of 8 November a fit on the 1000-iteration cap is read as the estimate, on V3's reading (the capped fits' speciation coefficients within 2% of the box span of the reference; extinction not identified); a fit on the clock stays budget-limited. cr and dd fits converge in minutes and are read wherever the rules allow.
