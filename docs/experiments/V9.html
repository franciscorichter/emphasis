Question

The ladder chooses nk on 54 of the 170 real trees, with a speciation factor above 1 on all 54. Processes with no \(K\) effect can still make some clades keep splitting: a heritable latent rate (ClaDS), protracted speciation, a fast region colonised rarely. If they make the ladder choose nk with factors like the real ones, nk on real trees reads as a heritable rate, not \(K\) in particular.

Design

generators latent rate (\(\sigma\) 0.10, 0.25, 0.40; trend-free), protracted speciation (duration 0.5, 2, 5), two regions (extirpation equal or none from A)
turnover 0.10 and 0.40
cells 16, each scale calibrated to a median of 100 tips (V9cal)
trees 30 a cell
fits the ladder's five models, as in V7; controls are V7's cr and nk cells
jobs 2400 (after 16 calibration jobs)

Pre-set rules

  1. A cell reproduces the K result when nk is chosen on more than 10% of read trees with a median factor per sd of \(K\) of at least 1.48.
  2. A cell with more than 20% of trees unread is sampler-limited.

Amendment 2026-11-01

The first calibration left 10 of 16 cells off the 100-tip target. The protracted-speciation generator had passed its parameters to PBD::pbd_sim in the wrong order (speciation completed at the extinction rate, incipient species split at the inverse of the mean duration); it now runs pbd_sim's own event loop with the order fixed and an explicit lineage cap, and returns the same trees as pbd_sim at the same seed (150 of 150). The search widens to scales 0.005-30 with 400 clades a step; a cell is calibrated when its median lies within 70-140 tips, and one that cannot be is reported with its size distribution and enters no rule. The latent rate at \(\sigma = 0.40\), whose surviving clades either die or pass the cap, stays and is read under that rule. The six calibrated cells (latent rate \(\sigma\) 0.10, the four two-region cells) keep their scales.

Recalibrated: 15 of 16 cells lie within 70-140 tips (all six protracted-speciation cells at 94-104; the latent rate at \(\sigma\) 0.25 at 84 and 84; at \(\sigma\) 0.40 and turnover 0.10 at 105). The latent rate at \(\sigma = 0.40\) and turnover 0.40 reaches a median of 35 tips with a third of its surviving clades over the cap: it is not calibrated and enters no rule.
