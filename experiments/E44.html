Question

Every estimation experiment so far mixed two things: whether the complete-data likelihood is that of the process the simulator runs, and how well Monte Carlo EM integrates over the hidden part. On a complete tree, every lineage kept, extinct ones included, nothing is hidden, so maximising the complete-data likelihood directly tests the first alone. Step 0.2 of the estimability plan. The D model was known to be defined two ways: the simulator held \(D\) constant between events, the likelihood held the clade mean \(M\) at its end-of-segment value so \(D\) grew within a segment (Open issues).

Design

models cr, dd, d, nd, ed, edc, ned, nedc, k, nk, exponential link
trees complete (extinct lineages kept), \(\rho = 1\), crown age 15, kept if both crown lineages survive and 60-160 extant tips
turnover 0.10 at the crown
effect the model's lineage-level coefficient at \(-0.35\) and \(+0.35\) per scale unit; single-covariate models in E24's unit (log(1 + effect) / sd of the covariate on cr clades, intercept calibrated), \(N\) models in E27's (\(\lambda_0 = 0.5\), \(\beta_N = \log 0.1 / K\), \(K\) calibrated)
trees per cell 40, the same trees before and after
fit the complete-data log-likelihood maximised by Subplex from the generating values and from a perturbed start, each restarted until a restart gains less than \(10^{-6}\); the two starts reached the same maximum on every tree

Pre-set rule

For every model and cell: the median ratio of each speciation coefficient to its generating value lies in [0.9, 1.1], and each slope has the right sign on at least 95% of trees. A model that fails has a likelihood that is not its simulator's and is corrected before the estimability study.

Bootstrap 95% intervals of the medians (a reading beside the rule, not a change to it): before the D change the d medians exclude 1 (0.70 [0.58, 0.78]; 1.46 [1.24, 1.65]) and so does nd -0.35 (0.77 [0.66, 0.91]); after it all four d/nd intervals contain 1 (d [0.86, 1.05] and [0.94, 1.40]; nd [0.91, 1.17] and [0.63, 1.25]). Raw ed excludes 1 in both cells (0.81 [0.74, 0.93]; 1.22 [1.02, 1.32]), both consistent with an additive +0.04 in \(\beta_{ED}\). The two D conventions differ by 0.40-1.20 nats per complete tree.

Conclusion

  • The D change was needed and brings d and nd to their generating values (emphasis 4e0b9dd); the d and nd +0.35 cells still miss the [0.9, 1.1] band at 40 trees, with intervals that contain 1. Every D experiment before 4e0b9dd (E19, E24's d arm, E30, E31, E37, \(D\) on the path in E20, E22, E33, and E40's nd fits) maximised the old convention's likelihood; the persistent \(\beta_D\) shortfall of those experiments is consistent with it.
  • Raw ED: no mismatch, a ridge and a window. An independent R computation of the ED likelihood from the definition equals eval_logf to \(3 \cdot 10^{-13}\), and an independent R simulator of the raw-ED process agrees with simulate_tree() (KS p 0.14-1 on 17 of 18 comparisons over 400 clades a cell). Pooled over 261 and 348 trees conditioned on survival only, the MLE returns \(\beta_{ED}\) at 1.005 and 1.002 of its value. What fails is the per-tree estimate: raw ED grows with time like the clade mean, so \(\beta_0\) and \(\beta_{ED}\) lie on a ridge and a single 100-tip tree places them along it (\(\beta_{ED}\) 0.93 and 1.17, \(\beta_0\) 1.20 and 1.07, with no selection on size). In the -0.35 cell E44's 60-160 tip window adds a selection bias: the generator's survivors have a median of 145 tips, so the window keeps the smaller quarter (pooled MLE on those trees \(\beta_0\) 1.25, \(\beta_{ED}\) 0.89). Centring removes the ridge, which is why edc and nedc pass. A regression test now holds the ED likelihood to the definition (emphasis e1c2e82). The diagnosis is in results/E44/ed-diagnosis/.
  • ned fails on sign share at a small effect in E27's unit (\(\beta\) -0.095 / +0.066): a power limit, its medians' intervals contain 1. k -0.35 fails on the intercept ratio because the generating intercept is -0.12; \(\beta_K\) recovers at 1.005. nk -0.35 fails because its generator stops at a median 73 tips, where \(\beta_N \approx -0.001\) is not identified.
