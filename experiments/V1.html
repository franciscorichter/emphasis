Question

A complete tree — every lineage kept, the extinct ones included — hides nothing, so maximising its complete-data likelihood tests whether that likelihood is the one of the process the simulator runs, with no Monte Carlo involved. E44 asked this with a 60–160-tip window on the trees, which biased the ed cells; V1 asks it again on the current build, with no window, at two sizes and two turnovers.

Design

models cr, dd, d, nd, ed, edc, ned, nedc, k, nk, exponential link
cells every reachable E43 cell at 100 and 200 tips: 64 cells
trees 40 a cell, complete, \(\rho = 1\), crown age 15, kept when both crown lineages survive; no size window
fit complete-data log-likelihood maximised by Subplex from the generating values and a perturbed start, restarted until a restart gains less than \(10^{-6}\)
jobs 2560, seconds each

Pre-set rule

Per cell, the median ratio of every speciation coefficient to its generating value lies in [0.9, 1.1], its bootstrap 95% interval contains 1, and a lineage-level coefficient has the right sign on at least 95% of trees. A cell that fails at both sizes is a likelihood that is not its simulator's; one that fails at 100 tips and passes at 200 is a size effect.

Results

The registered rule holds in 29 of 64 cells (sim/V1-analysis.R, results/V1/coefficients.csv).

  • cr passes at both sizes and turnovers; edc in 7 of 8 cells; k at \(-0.35\) in all; nk at turnover 0.10.
  • The cells that fail split two ways. Single-covariate models miss by 5–10% on the intercept or the slope (d, ed), or on the sign share of k at \(+0.35\) (0.78–0.93). The \(N\) models at turnover 0.40 (dd, nd, ned, nedc) read \(\beta_N\) at 1.1–1.4 of its value and the intercept 5–10% high.
  • The per-tree rule reads a 100- or 200-tip maximum, which carries small-sample error, on trees selected for survival and for staying under the lineage cap. A pooled estimate per cell (one parameter vector for the cell's 40 trees, sim/V1-pooled.R, a diagnostic beside the rule, not a change to it) separates the two: small-sample error averages out of it, selection does not.

Pooled estimate (diagnostic, results/V1/pooled.csv). 138 of the 150 coefficients lie within [0.9, 1.1] of their generating values, and almost no clade was dropped at the lineage cap (a share of 0 in the \(N\)-model cells at turnover 0.40, 0.02 elsewhere), so the cap does not explain the failures and most per-tree failures are small-sample error. Outside [0.9, 1.1]: \(\beta_N\) of dd at turnover 0.40 (1.17, 1.15), of nd (1.21, 0.86), ned (0.85) and nedc (1.14, 1.15); \(\beta_D\) of d and nd (1.10–1.16) in three cells; \(\beta_{ED}\) of ned (0.85, 1.16). These are what is left once 40 trees are pooled, on trees kept only when both crown lineages survive.
