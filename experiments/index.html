These are the pre-registered experiments behind the emphasis package: each tests the Monte Carlo EM estimator against an exact answer or a generating value, or asks what a dated tree can identify. There are 47 experiment pages, and two more runs are planned. Find a question in the tables below, read its status against the vocabulary, and open the page for the design, the rule and the full results; the conclusions that hold now are collected in What we know and the terms in the Glossary.

Reading the status

status meaning
Holds the conclusion is a current result
Holds within a scope it is a current result within the scope its page states
Partly holds part of it is a current result; the page says which part
Superseded by E<n> the result will be replaced when E<n> reports; until then it is the measurement on record
Historical a method-development step, not cited as a result
Merged into E<n> its record is carried by another page's table
Running started; results so far are on its page
Planned registered or designed, not started

Conflicts between the record and the data are listed in Open issues.

Reading a page

Every experiment page has the same headings: Question, Design, Pre-set rule (as registered before the data existed), Results, Conclusion, Related. Variants (E8b, E10b, E12b, E19b, E24b, E34r, E36c and others) are sections of the parent page. Three markings recur:

  • sampler-limited: the cell's median effective sample is below 10; its rates are reported, not read against the rule.
  • clock-limited: the wall clock ended more than 10% of the cell's fits; its rates are floors.
  • unfinished: the fits ran to their iteration cap without meeting the stopping rule.

5. Extinction, conditioning and what a tree carries
