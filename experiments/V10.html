Question

Extinction is read from absences. Whether a tree identifies it depends on turnover and size; the conditioned fits of E45 and E46 and the exact map of E38 already hold the answer, once read together.

Design

inputs E45's cr, dd fits (25–800 tips; turnover 0.10, 0.25, 0.40); E46's extinction intercepts and slopes; E38's exact profiles
new fits none

Pre-set rules

  1. Per model, turnover and size: is the extinction rate reliable (E46's rule 2)?
  2. Is a slope generated at 0 fitted at 0?
  3. Does MCEM's extinction estimate track the exact one?
