Question

The path selects covariates from one draw set without fitting each model. Earlier runs drew at the generating values (unavailable to a user) or read \(D\) from the old likelihood; E33 at a fitted estimate failed.

Design

trees V7's
draws 3000 by thinning at V7's dd estimate, the 150 of largest weight kept
path covariate_path() over \(N\), \(D\), ED, \(K\), speciation and extinction, BIC
jobs 640, each run once V7's dd fit of its tree is written

Pre-set rules

  1. On cr and dd trees no lineage-level covariate is active at BIC's optimum on at least 90%.
  2. On each lineage-level generator's trees its covariate is active on at least 80%.
