Question

The likelihood conditioned on survival needs \(P(S \mid \theta, T, \rho)\), the probability that both crown lineages leave a sampled descendant. It does not depend on the observed tree, so one function per model, crown age and sampling fraction serves every fit of a cell. Its accuracy was measured only for cr and dd over the whole box (E36c), and its variability between trainings never. Steps 0.3 and 0.4 of the estimability plan.

Design

(a) Generators for the estimability study, calibrated so the median size of surviving trees equals each target (50, 100, 200, 400), no size window: ten models, turnover 0.10 and 0.40, effects \(\pm 0.35\). (b) Per model, a box spanning the calibrated generating values, widened; 200 points in it. (c) At each point \(P(S)\) by direct simulation (a clade reaching the lineage cap of 2000 counts as surviving); for cr and dd also the exact value. (d) The package's survival GAM trained on 2000, 5000 and 20 000 clades, and retrained with 20 seeds.

Pre-set rule

An emulator is adequate if, at the 200 points, the 90th percentile of \(|\log\hat P - \log P_{ref}|\) is at most 0.05 and the median retraining standard deviation of \(\log\hat P\) at most 0.02; the smallest adequate size is used, else direct simulation. cr and dd use exact functions. Amendment before any reference run: a point's reference stops once the standard error of its \(\log P\) is at most 0.01 (2000 to 20 000 clades), because 20 000 clades everywhere would take six days, mostly at points with \(P \approx 1\).

Results

So far: the calibration (results/E43/calibration.csv) and the trained emulators (2000 and 5000 clades with 20 seeds, 20 000 with one, 2026-10-17), whose accuracy waits on the Monte Carlo reference (1287 of 1600 points at 2026-10-18). Targets some generators cannot reach: dd, nd, nedc +0.35 at turnover 0.40 and 400 tips (the \(N\) models keep \(\lambda_0 = 0.5\) and stop at 250-290 tips); ned -0.35 at turnover 0.40, and at 0.10 and 400 tips; nk -0.35 from 100 tips at 0.10 and at every size at 0.40; k -0.35 at 0.40 (at most 14 tips). The exact survival functions match simulation of \(10^5\) clades: cr \(\log P\) -1.7222 against -1.7252 (SE 0.0068), dd -1.9920 against -1.9993 (SE 0.0080). The dd function was later reduced exactly to a chain on \(N\) alone (100-1000 times faster; E45).

Conclusion

Not yet evaluable. The unreachable targets constrain the design of the lineage-level study (E46).
