Question

Since emphasis 4e0b9dd (2026-10-15) the likelihood holds \(D\) constant between events, as the simulator does (E44). Every nd fit of E26, E28 and E40 was made under the earlier likelihood, which the package no longer computes; the choice tables, E40's pairs rule and E41's reading of nd edges rest on those fits. E48 refits the nd rung alone and rereads them. Step 0.8 of the estimability plan.

Design

trees three sets, each with its own stored fits: E40's 170 trees with a stored fit (the five stopped trees left out), E28's 29 families, and the 18 families of E26 not in E28; 217 in all
reading of a tree as sim/E25-real.R reads it: binary, tip edges extended to the deepest tip, at its set's sampling fraction
families in several sets refitted once per set, from that set's own dd fit
model nd on the exponential link at the tree's \(\rho\)
box auto_bounds(model = "nd") on the tree
draws, iterations 300 draws, 300 iterations as the budget
clock four hours per fit; one tree per process with a hard limit of six hours, so that an E-step that does not return ends as a recorded hang
start nested from the stored dd estimate, as estimate_rates(model = "all") starts it
ranking per tree, compare_models() on the stored cr, dd, ned and nk fits with the new nd fit in place of the stored one; the stored ranking recorded beside it

The stored rungs' log-likelihoods come from builds whose likelihood for models without \(D\) is the current one: the changes since f30a791 that touch the likelihood concern \(D\) only.

Pre-set rule

  1. A new nd fit is read when its effective sample is at least 10 and it did not end on its clock; otherwise it is sampler-limited or clock-limited, counted and not read. A tree stopped by the hard limit is reported as hung.
  2. The choice tables, per set and for E28 and E26 merged as in the biology paper: the parsimonious choice (fewest parameters within 2 AIC of the minimum) first and the minimum-AIC model beside it, over the trees whose chosen fit has an effective sample of at least 10, with the number of trees whose choice changes from the stored one in each direction. If the parsimonious choice changes on more than 10% of a set's readable trees, that set's choice table in the papers and on the site is replaced by E48's; otherwise the tables stand with the nd row updated.
  3. E40's pairs rule recomputed with the new nd fits, thresholds unchanged: agreement on the parsimonious choice on at least 70% of the pairs where both chosen fits are read, and on the sign of each lineage-level coefficient read in both on at least 80%; fewer than 10 readable pairs is not evaluable.
  4. E41's reading of edges: a parameter of a new nd fit within \(10^{-6}\) of the box span of a bound is on an edge, reported as not bounded by the tree in that direction, and not read; the share of read nd fits with \(\beta_D\) or \(\gamma_D\) on an edge is reported.
  5. The nd coefficient: over read fits with \(\beta_D\) off its edges, the number with \(\beta_D < 0\) and the factor per standard deviation of \(D\), replacing the counts the biology paper quotes from the stored fits.

Results

Running. At 2026-10-20 00:43, 3 of 217 trees written (two E40 bird clades and one E28 family, 50 to 53 tips, each nd fit at its 300-iteration budget in 485 to 518 s with an effective sample of 122 to 286; the parsimonious choice unchanged on all three). Nothing is read before the run ends.

The stored values it rereads: E28's parsimonious choice cr 4, dd 5, nd 0, ned 8, nk 8 over 25 usable families; the 46-family merged table, 36 usable, 21 lineage-level by minimum AIC (nk 12, ned 9), median D factor 0.83 over 24 read nd fits; E40's 121 of 170 trees with a lineage-level parsimonious choice, nd on 3.

Conclusion

Not yet evaluable.
