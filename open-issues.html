Open issues

What is still open in the package and in the record, each with the page that gives the evidence and what closes it. Work that is registered or running (E45, E48, E6b, E10c, E46, E47) is listed on the experiments index; each experiment page states what its record does not hold.

Decisions waiting on a measurement

  • The relaxation default of the surrogate's mean field. E10b's rule favours a fixed relaxation of ⅛ over "auto" (107 of 139 diverging units converge against 77), but it was measured at 200 and 1000 sweeps, and the package ships 20 sweeps at \(\rho = 1\) and 200 below. E10c (on the E10 page) measures both settings at the shipped budget and decides; until it reports, the package keeps damping = "auto" and every fit records the relaxation it used in its notes.

Package

  • The M-step is not yet bounded by the clock. The wall clock (max_time) binds inside the E-step and both samplers, so a fit whose E-step does not return stops on time; the optimiser of the M-step still runs to its own tolerance, and on a 181-tip tree it is the larger part of an iteration. A fix that passes the remaining time to the optimiser is being tested.
