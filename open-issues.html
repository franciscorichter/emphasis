Decisions that wait on the author, defects in the package, places where the written record disagrees with the data, and what the repository does not record. Each entry names the page that gives the evidence. In every disagreement below, the experiment pages follow the data files.

Decisions for the author

  • Relaxation default. E10b's pre-set rule is met (a fixed ⅛ converges 107 of 139 diverging units at 1000 sweeps, "auto" 77), so the rule changes the default to ⅛; the package still ships damping = "auto". E10
  • The ED-aware thinning ordering. It fails rule C4 (ED-aware ≥ mean-field on 77% of trees against 95%) and remains the default thinning variant for ED and K models. E3
  • Which family split to quote. For the 29 families of E28 the parsimonious choice gives ned 8, nk 8 among the 18 lineage-level verdicts (plus cr on 2), the minimum AIC gives ned 8, nk 10; the merged-table counts (21: nk 12, ned 9) use the minimum-AIC winner. The record does not say which a paper quotes. E28, E26
  • Whether relaxation is reported to the caller. Raised by E10's design; no decision is recorded. E10
  • The supplement's reading of E35 (a). It calls the turnover-0.25 negative K slope "the first extinction slope recovered in the study"; by the pre-set rule the slope is "not identified" (positive arm sampler-limited). E35

Package defects

  • A fit its clock cannot stop. The wall clock is read only between iterations, so a fit whose E-step never returns runs on: E30's nd-em035-k03 ran 60 hours with a four-hour max_time and was stopped by hand. E30
  • The likelihood read \(D\) differently from the simulator on a segment (fixed). The likelihood held the clade mean pendant age \(M\) at its segment-end value while the simulator held \(D\) constant between events; emphasis 4e0b9dd makes the likelihood hold \(D\) constant too, and E44 measures the effect on complete trees: before the change d returned \(\beta_D\) at 0.70 and 1.46 of its value, after it 0.95 and 1.16. Every D experiment before 4e0b9dd (E19, E30, E31, E37, \(D\) on the path, E40's nd fits) maximised the old likelihood.
  • simulate_tree's max_tries. It counts attempts after the first, so the default of 1 draws twice and completes 0.77 of constant-rate runs where one draw completes 0.52; comparisons of completion rates need max_tries = 0. E14
  • Survival emulator label (fixed). Clades passing the lineage cap were labelled extinct; fixed in d3dee92 (labelled survived, 2000 clades). E36 (b) was not rerun after the fix. E34r (E34) confirms E34's verdict under it.
  • Raw-ED simulator (fixed). Before f30a791 the forward simulator held rates fixed between events, giving ed clades 30% too large; E24b reruns ed. Trees drawn from the same seeds differ before and after f30a791, so E29/E31 are not E27's trees and E30 is not E19b's. E24, E29, E30
  • Two figures print NA. fig-clades-effects prints "NA of 32 families" and an empty NA panel; fig-clades-shape's subtitle carries an NA (a missing label in the figure script). E26
  • E26-analysis.R has no refusal for tip spread. Macropodidae (tip spread 19%) was fitted and appears in the merged table (dd, lead over cr 32.6), although the design refuses trees above 15%. E26
