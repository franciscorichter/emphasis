Description

One denominator for both proposals. Three kinds of draw carry weight zero and all three belong in it: a draw whose log-weight is -Inf because the model gives its tree probability zero; a draw the proposal rejected for a reason that depends on the parameters – a missing lineage still alive at the present under complete sampling, whose density under \(f\) is zero; and nothing else. A draw rejected for a reason that does not depend on the parameters, an overrun of the missing-lineage budget, is not a sample and stays out.

Usage

.is_summary(lw, n_zero_weight = 0L, n_rejected = 0L)

Arguments

lw

Log-weights of the completed draws.

n_zero_weight

Completed draws whose weight is zero.

n_rejected

Draws rejected for a parameter-dependent reason.

Details

The two proposals reached this denominator by different routes – the thinning path through n_zero_weight, the conditional path through a separate acceptance factor – and the difference between the two routes is the size of the effect they are used to measure.

Value

A list: fhat, ess, n_finite, n_den.
