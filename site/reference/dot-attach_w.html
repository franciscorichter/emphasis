Description

The proposal's aggregate rate already carries the clade mean of the covariate, so a lineage's own rate is that aggregate plus its own departure from the mean, lam + beta * (c_s - mean(c)) – not lam + beta * c_s, which shifts every lineage instead of spreading them and leaves the mean in twice.

Usage

.attach_w(cov, lam, beta)

Arguments

cov

Per-lineage covariate values (here the pendant age).

lam

The aggregate rate the mean field supplies.

beta

The covariate's coefficient.

Details

Weights are floored away from zero rather than at it. A lineage the model gives a positive rate must stay reachable, or the proposal no longer covers the target and the estimate is biased rather than merely noisy. The floor is a thousandth of the aggregate, which costs a bounded amount of weight variance and keeps the support.

Value

A positive weight per lineage, averaging lam.
