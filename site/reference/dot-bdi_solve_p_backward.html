Description

The ODE itself carries no sampling fraction; rho enters only as the terminal condition p(tp) = rho, which is the probability that a lineage alive at the present leaves a sampled descendant (itself). That is the whole of the incomplete-sampling change on the backward side, and it reproduces the closed form of .bdi_p_cr under constant rates.

Usage

.bdi_solve_p_backward(
  pars8,
  model_bin,
  link,
  bt,
  tp,
  Nhat_fun,
  Phat_fun,
  Ehat_fun,
  t_grid,
  rho = 1
)
