Description

Birth-death models of species diversification whose speciation and extinction rates depend on covariates the process generates: the number of lineages \(N\), the age-imbalance \(D\), the evolutionary distinctiveness \(ED\) and the number of ancestral splits \(K\). A reconstructed phylogeny records only the extant species; the likelihood is integrated over the unobserved extinct (and, at incomplete sampling, unsampled) lineages by importance sampling on augmented trees.

Details

The main functions:

  • simulate_tree: forward simulation under a model, and augmentation of an observed tree.

  • estimate_rates: maximum-likelihood estimation by Monte Carlo expectation-maximization ("mcem"), the cross-entropy method ("cem") or a GAM fitted to a likelihood surface ("gam").

  • compare_models: AIC comparison of fits that accounts for their Monte Carlo error.

  • lineage_table, covariate_path: the per-lineage design of a tree and a selection path over the covariates.

  • diagnose_mcem, diagnose_cem, diagnose_gam: convergence and importance-sampling diagnostics.

Computation is in C++ through Rcpp and RcppParallel; every function runs on one thread unless num_threads is set.
