Description

The sampler conditions on a rate that is a function of the lineage count alone: .bdi_p_cr and .bdi_solve_p_backward solve one survival probability p(t) shared by every lineage alive at t, and the aggregate Gillespie of .bdi_augment_one moves n_alive lineages with one pair of rates. That is what an N-only model gives it.

Usage

.bdi_supported(model_bin, link, rho = 1)

Arguments

model_bin

Length-3 binary model vector c(use_N, use_M, use_D).

link

Integer link code: 0 linear, 1 exponential.

rho

Sampling fraction in (0, 1]. Default 1.

Details

A lineage-level model (D, model_bin[3] != 0, or ED, model_bin[4] != 0) gives each lineage its own rate, so no single p(t) is the model's. The sampler then conditions on the rate of the clade's average lineage instead: an ED of P-hat/N-hat, which the mean-field iteration solves for, and a D of zero, since D is centred. The proposal is exact for that surrogate model and the importance weights carry each lineage's departure from the average; the attachment of a birth is tilted by the parent's pendant age, where D and ED read the tree. This is available on the linear and exponential links. The M covariate (model_bin[2]) is not in scope: the sampler's own mean-field state would have to be closed on P as well as N, which is written but not gated.

The linear and exponential dd rates are monotone in N. On a 20-tip tree over a 4 x 8 x 4 grid in (beta_0, beta_N, rho) with a 200-sweep budget, the Picard iteration of .bdi_iterate reached the fixed point for both in every cell, in at most 27 iterations at rho = 1. It is slower below it: an exponential dd cell needs 63 sweeps at rho = 0.2, and converging linear dd cells have needed 126 and 134, which is what sets the rho < 1 budget.

Incomplete sampling is in scope for every model and link the gate otherwise accepts. p(t) becomes the probability of leaving a sampled descendant (.bdi_p_cr, .bdi_solve_p_backward, both of which take rho), and .bdi_to_tree_df emits the 5e10 sentinel for a missing lineage still alive at the present, so the draws carry unsampled extant lineages and N(t) counts them.
