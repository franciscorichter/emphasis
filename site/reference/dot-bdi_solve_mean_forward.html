Description

State: s = (m, P_miss) with dm/dt = (lam_bdi - mu_bdi) * m + nu (self-consistent BDI mean) dP_miss/dt = m (mean-field: each alive missing lineage extends its pendant edge at rate 1)

Usage

.bdi_solve_mean_forward(
  pars8,
  model_bin,
  link,
  bt,
  tp,
  p_fun,
  Nhat_fun,
  Phat_fun,
  Ehat_fun,
  t_grid
)

Details

Rates lam, mu are evaluated at (N̂, P̂, Ê) to support DD/PD/EP + mixed.

Value

list(m_vals, pm_vals) on t_grid.
