# dev/

Working material, excluded from the package build by `.Rbuildignore`.

- `audit/` — the as-implemented map, the 105 hypotheses, the findings, and one reproduction
  script per finding.
- `simulator/` — `agreement.R` checks the browser simulator (`simulator.html`, engine
  `assets/simulator/emphasis-sim.js`) against `simulate_tree()`: its ED routine against
  `ed_fair_proportion()` tip by tip, and 2,000 surviving trees per scenario from each simulator
  over ten model × link scenarios, compared on nine summaries (z of the difference in means, KS
  on the extant-tip count). `agreement.csv` is the last full run. Run it after any change to
  `general_tree.hpp`, `ed_covariate.hpp` or the engine.
- `validation/` — the MLE-recovery study: design, scripts, reference gate. A tier's trees,
  results, figures and tables are regenerated by `run.sh` and are not tracked.
- `crdd_invariance.R` — the cr/dd bit-for-bit gate. `capture` fixes a corpus of augmented trees
  and their `eval_logf` scores from whichever build is loaded; `compare` rescores that corpus
  from the build under test and requires `identical()`. Augmentation takes a seed from R since
  wave 2, so a fixed seed reproduces a draw at `num_threads = 1`; the capture/compare workflow
  stays the right one across builds, because a change to the sampler changes which trees are
  drawn even under the same seed,
  so the corpus itself has to cross the two builds; `dev/.crdd_corpus.rds` is not tracked.
- `bdi_invariance.R` — the check that recording parents in the BDI sampler leaves its random
  stream and every draw unchanged; `bdi_invariance.rds` is its capture.
- `mfc_mesh.R` — re-evaluating the frozen rates of the BDI sampler's Gillespie branch on a
  mesh: the weights tighten and the estimate does not move (the `mesh` entry of
  `estimate_rates()`'s control documentation); `mfc_mesh.csv` is the run.
- `simcheck_ed.R` — two builds of the forward simulator compared on raw-ED generators (tip
  count, gamma, Colless, median branching time, survival over 400 surviving clades), cited
  by experiment E24.
