# dev/

Working material, excluded from the package build by `.Rbuildignore`.

- `audit/` — the as-implemented map, the 105 hypotheses, the findings, and one reproduction
  script per finding.
- `validation/` — the MLE-recovery study: design, scripts, reference gate. A tier's trees,
  results, figures and tables are regenerated by `run.sh` and are not tracked.
- `pendant_*.R` — the scripts that compared the pendant-age covariates before and after the
  topology change. `pendant_baseline.R` captures a baseline from whichever build is loaded and
  `pendant_compare.R` diffs two captures; the `.rds` captures themselves are build-specific and
  are not tracked, so regenerate them against the two commits being compared.
- `crdd_invariance.R` — the cr/dd bit-for-bit gate. `capture` fixes a corpus of augmented trees
  and their `eval_logf` scores from whichever build is loaded; `compare` rescores that corpus
  from the build under test and requires `identical()`. Augmentation takes a seed from R since
  wave 2, so a fixed seed reproduces a draw at `num_threads = 1`; the capture/compare workflow
  stays the right one across builds, because a change to the sampler changes which trees are
  drawn even under the same seed,
  so the corpus itself has to cross the two builds; `dev/.crdd_corpus.rds` is not tracked.
- `proposal_gates.R` — the measurements behind `tests/testthat/test-proposal-density.R`
  at sample sizes a test cannot afford: the KS test of the drawn lifetimes against the
  density `sampling_prob` charges them with (`lifetimes`), the effective sample size of a
  D-model fit (`ess`), and `E_q[f/q]` against a brute-force marginal likelihood on a 2-tip
  tree (`unbiased`).
- `gates.R` — prints the measured agreements behind `tests/testthat/test-d-compensator.R`:
  the ep_linear compensator against numerical integration, the beta_D = 0 reduction, and the
  pendant PD `Model::nh_rate` reads.
- `bench_augment.R` — augmentation cost against tree size, for the pendant-PD scan H53 named.
